torch.nn.init.normal_

Official docs URL: https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/generated/torch.nn.init.normal_.html

torch.nn.init.normal_(tensor: torch.Tensor, mean: float = 0.0, std: float = 1.0, generator: torch._C.Generator | None = None) -> torch.Tensor

Fill the input Tensor with values drawn from the normal distribution.
mathcalN(mean, std^2).
Args:
    tensor: an n-dimensional `torch.Tensor`
    mean: the mean of the normal distribution
    std: the standard deviation of the normal distribution
    generator: the torch Generator to sample from (default: None)
Examples:
    >>> w = torch.empty(3, 5)
    >>> nn.init.normal_(w)
